Our Story

Welcome to a cozy, creative space located on the first floor of a beautiful house filled with live plants. We pride ourselves on offering a classy ambiance with nice food, delicious burgers, and good service.

Beyond food, we are a hub for creativity - hosting workshops and featuring a small open stage where anyone can sing, perform, and share their art with the community.
